First-year M.S. student at Kyung Hee University, working on intelligent metasurface-assisted ISAC. I specialize in deep learning and convex optimization and have extensive experience with applications of deep learning and optimization theory to wireless communication systems, including MIMO-OFDM channel prediction, sum-rate and max-min-rate optimization for ISAC systems. I have collaborated on funded research with groups at the University of Quebec, and Mid Sweden University, Sweden. My research has resulted in several peer-reviewed publications in leading IEEE venues.

    Experience

    1. Intelligent Communications Systems Lab, Kyung Hee University

      Mar 2026 – Present

      Graduate Research Assistant · Intelligent Metasurface-Assisted Wireless Systems

      • Proposed an RSMA-based, stacked-intelligent-metasurface-assisted downlink, optimized with block-coordinate gradient ascent: 33% higher sum rate than the SDMA baseline while meeting the sensing beampattern constraint.
      • Extended it to a max-min fairness design, improving the minimum user rate by 8% over SDMA.
      • Co-developed an SCA-based algorithm for RSMA with active STAR-RIS under nonlinear amplification: 19% higher sum rate than SDMA.

      MATLAB · CVX · SCA · Gradient-based & non-linear optimization · Wave-domain beamforming

    2. Laboratory of Signal and System Integration, Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières

      May 2025 – Dec 2025

      Research Assistant · Continual Learning for MIMO-OFDM Channel Prediction

      • Generated a 3GPP TR 38.901 CDL-D 8×2 MIMO-OFDM CSI dataset with Doppler-induced channel aging to emulate non-stationary channels.
      • Built an LSTM channel predictor with Monte-Carlo dropout for uncertainty estimation, trained with a heteroscedastic uncertainty-weighted loss.
      • Proposed uncertainty-weighted experience replay with LARS buffer replacement, keeping per-RB NMSE flat with replay memory under 5% of the dataset.

      Python · PyTorch · MATLAB 5G Toolbox · 3GPP TR 38.901

    3. Information Processing and Transmission Lab, NUST

      May 2024 – Dec 2024

      Research Assistant · Safety-Critical 5G URLLC for Industrial IoT

      • Modeled a 5G NR industrial uplink with configured-grant routine traffic and slot-level preemptive access for safety-critical devices, with Mid Sweden University and Manchester Metropolitan University.
      • Derived a closed-form end-to-end safety response time model (IEC 61784-3-3) linking HARQ retransmissions, BLER, and slot alignment to system-level delay.
      • Implemented custom HARQ entities in the MATLAB 5G Toolbox, showing over 50% lower response time as subcarrier spacing grows from 15 to 60 kHz.

      MATLAB 5G Toolbox · 3GPP NR PHY/MAC · HARQ · Stochastic modeling

    4. Optimization and Machine Learning (OPTIMAL) Lab, NUST

      May 2023 – May 2024

      Research Assistant · Deep Learning for Signal Denoising and Classification

      • Designed a lightweight 1D CNN on Mel-spectrograms for 7-class lung-sound classification, reaching 99.9% accuracy on ICBHI 2017.
      • Built a U-Net-style denoising autoencoder with STFT spectral masking and transfer learning, reaching 95.5% accuracy in a real-time mobile pipeline.
      • Proposed a GAN with spatial-channel attention for denoising: 2 dB higher PSNR with 2M fewer parameters.

      Python · CNNs · Autoencoders · GANs · STFT
